> **Prompt.** Lennox invests \$2,566.70 today. The project returns 10%. Cash inflows are \$325, \$400, \$550, X, \$750, \$800 in years 1–6. Find X.

## Intuition

"Returns 10%" means **NPV at 10% equals zero**. The \$2,566.70 paid out today must equal the present value of all six inflows discounted at 10%.

Strategy:
1. Discount the five **known** inflows to today and sum them.
2. Subtract that sum from \$2,566.70 — what's left is the PV of the year-4 inflow.
3. Multiply by (1.10)<sup>4</sup> to get <em>X</em> itself (the dollar amount in year 4, not its PV).

## Step by step

```js
const known = [325, 400, 550, /*year 4 = X*/, 750, 800];
const pvKnown =
  325/Math.pow(1+r, 1) +
  400/Math.pow(1+r, 2) +
  550/Math.pow(1+r, 3) +
  750/Math.pow(1+r, 5) +
  800/Math.pow(1+r, 6);
const pvX = inv - pvKnown;
const X = pvX * Math.pow(1+r, 4);
```
