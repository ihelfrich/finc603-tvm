# Q4 — Back Out the APR

> **Prompt.** Deposit \$800 at the end of each month for 24 months. The account ends at \$20,951. What APR (compounded monthly) did you earn?

## Intuition

Payment, count, ending balance — known. Rate — unknown. Whenever the rate is the unknown, reach for `RATE`.

The trap most people fall into: `RATE` returns the **per-period rate**, not the annual rate. The period here is a month, so multiply by 12 to get APR. Stop early and you'll answer 0.75% instead of 9%.

## Step by step

```js
const monthlyRate = rateFromFV(fv, pmt, nper);
const apr = monthlyRate * 12;
const fvCheck = fvAnnuity(pmt, monthlyRate, nper);
```

```js
formulaBar(`=RATE(${nper}, -${pmt}, 0, ${fv})`, {result: fmtPct(monthlyRate, 4) + " per month"})
```
```js
formulaBar(`APR = ${(monthlyRate*100).toFixed(4)}% × 12`, {result: fmtPct(apr, 2)})
```
```js
formulaBar(`Verify:  =-FV(${(monthlyRate*100).toFixed(4)}%, ${nper}, ${pmt})`, {result: fmt$(fvCheck)})
```
