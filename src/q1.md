# Q1 — Annuity Due with a New Rate

> **Prompt.** An ordinary annuity of \$200/year is worth \$555 at 4%. Turn it into an annuity *due* and drop the rate to 2%. By how much does PV change?

## Intuition

Two forces, both pushing PV **up**.

1. **Annuity due** shifts every payment one period earlier. Earlier dollars are worth more today.
2. **Lower rate** discounts future dollars less aggressively.

Both effects raise PV. The answer must be an increase — that already kills (a), (c), and (e). What's left is the *size* of the increase.

To re-price, we need the number of payments. Solve for <em>N</em> from the original information.

## Step 1 — solve for <em>N</em>

```js
const Nraw = nper(pv0, r1, pmt);
const N = Math.max(1, Math.round(Nraw));
```
